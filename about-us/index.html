About your Coach

 Although your coaches are certified in a number of areas, 
they have gone through many life's experiences of their own.  
This also enables them to help you move forward on paths where they have once been.
Kevin Bergman
Sherée Bergman
You may click on their names above for more information.
Kevin and Sherée are the founders and facilitators of Heart to Heart Life Coaching LLC located in the Chicagoland area.
​They work out of an office in Algonquin, Illinois under Aquila Christian International.

They are Empowerment Coaches in the areas of Marriage/Relationship/Family Restoration,
Christ-Centered Discipleship, Emotional Healing, Personal Productivity and Life Purpose.
Sherée also works with Abused Women.

The Holy Spirit has led Kevin and Sherée, through their years of training,
to a blended and effective coaching style from all that they have learned and lived.

If you would like to get to know them in person,
they are usually at any services or events that are held at Aquila Christian International/Healing of America.

God is love. Whoever lives in love lives in God, and God in them.


Coaches at Heart to Heart Life Coaching LLC are NOT Licensed Professional Counselors 
and do NOT do counseling nor are they Mental Health Providers. 
They do NOT make diagnoses of any physical or mental health issues. 
They are Christian coaches who pray for a person's needs 
from a spiritual perspective based on the teachings of the Bible.
Counseling focuses on looking in the "rearview mirror"
​and Life Coaching focuses on looking through the "windshield".  
However, occasionally coaches do have to direct you to the rearview mirror to help you move forward.
