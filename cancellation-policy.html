Cancellation/Reschedule of Appointment Policy

We would appreciate at least a 48-72-hour notice for any cancellations or schedule changes. 

We understand cancellations and appointment changes can happen.
However, appointments cancelled/changed less than 12 hours of a scheduled appointment time
will incur a cancellation charge which will be the full fee of your scheduled appointment time.

If you are canceling or rescheduling your appointment more than 12 hours before 
your scheduled appointment time, you may do so at button below
​if this is the way you originally set up your appointment.
Cancel or Reschedule your Appointment here
Any appointment cancellations/rescheduling left on the company’s voice mail, text message and/or email ​will not be considered an appointment cancellation.  
​We are very sorry but there is no exceptions to this 
Cancellation/Reschedule of Appointment Policy.
