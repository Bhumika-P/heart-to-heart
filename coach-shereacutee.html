About

Coach Sherée

Sherée Bergman is a certified life coach, speaker, and spiritual mentor who helps people work through emotional wounds, recognize unhealthy patterns, and deepen their relationship with God. At the heart of her work is a simple conviction: no story is beyond God’s redemption, and painful experiences do not have to define the future.

Through Heart to Heart Life Coaching, Sherée combines biblical principles with practical coaching tools in a welcoming, grace-filled setting. She helps clients explore how childhood experiences, relationships with caregivers, and unresolved hurt may influence their beliefs, choices, and relationships today. Her approach encourages understanding, self-compassion, healthy boundaries, and meaningful steps toward emotional and spiritual growth.

Sherée’s commitment to this work comes from her own life. Childhood emotional neglect and family dysfunction left wounds she carried into adulthood. Her journey has included toxic relationships, chronic emotional abuse, divorce, single parenting, brain surgery, temporary blindness, and the traumatic loss of her mother. Through these experiences, her relationship with Jesus Christ became the source of healing, strength, and hope.

That journey shapes how she walks alongside others. Sherée understands the courage it takes to acknowledge painful experiences, question familiar patterns, and begin making changes. She offers space for clients to feel heard and understood while exploring the next steps in their own healing journey.

Sherée is the founder of Heart to Heart Life Coaching in the Chicagoland area. Her professional credentials include certifications in Life Coaching, Christian Life Coaching, Relationship Coaching, Relationship Communication, Grief and Joy Restoration, HeartMath Coaching, and Christian Counseling. As an ordained minister and chaplain, she brings spiritual discernment and pastoral care to her coaching, creating a holistic approach that addresses the emotional, relational, and spiritual needs of those she serves.

Her books—Start at Start to Finish Strong, Start at Start Transformation Guide, Start at Start Breakthrough Journal, and I Can’t Love You Through the Fence—explore emotional wounds, the importance of feeling safe in relationships, and restoration through faith in Christ. Through her writing, workshops, speaking engagements, and support group, she encourages people to recognize limiting beliefs, understand their responses to stress, and build healthier ways of relating to themselves and others.

Sherée lives by the promise of Romans 8:28: “And we know that in all things God works for the good of those who love Him, who have been called according to His purpose” (NIV). This scripture is more than a belief—it’s the foundation of her story and the reason she can confidently guide others through their own.

Sherée lives in Algonquin, Illinois, and is the mother of three adult children. She enjoys spending time with family and friends, writing, decorating, organizing, crafting, biking, glamping, and quiet walks with her two small dogs. Her interest in dog training, shaped by years working as a surgical technician in an animal clinic, continues today as she trains her Yorkshire Terrier in agility.

Above all, Sherée treasures time spent sitting with the Lord and believes that God is the Great Healer. Her desire is to help others move forward with the assurance that their past does not disqualify them from a life of hope, connection, and purpose.
