OPEN-HEART COACHING
We have all been wounded at some point in our life. 
As children, we just internalize these wounds.  As adults, we try to dismiss or bury them.
Not addressing these hurts alter our thinking, our relationships and our life's path. 
Open-Heart Coaching or Emotional Healing is God's gift to us. 
It helps us find freedom from the emotional aftermath of hurtful, harmful or traumatic experiences.
​Because it is Holy Spirit given, it is like going through many counseling sessions in a very short period of time. 
This type of coaching is lead by the Holy Spirit and the Lord does the healing.
​It is truly life changing!  
Click on button above for more information.
During our sessions, we will listen to your words and heart, ask questions, 
​gently speak the truth and share insights about possibilities and obstacles to help speed your growth and development. 
Think of us as your partner for success.

A coach does not give advice or diagnosis. 
A coach provides guidance, encouragement, creative ideas, support and accountability.

Life Coaching Sessions are in-person for locals or Phone/Skype/Zoom​ for locals, out-of-town and International clients.
Invest in Yourself
It's best not to let too much time get in-between appointments.
Suggested times: Once a week
This will give you time to internalize your last session, do any homework that was given, 
and not get too far from your last session so you don't loose your momentum.
If once a week can not be achieved, it is suggested not to let appointments span out longer than bi-weekly.

Sessions must be paid for before actual appointment.

A detailed profile must be filled out and sent back to us before your appointment.
To receive a profile please call the office.

After you receive the profile, complete it and either fax (this is a private fax) or mail it back to our office.
