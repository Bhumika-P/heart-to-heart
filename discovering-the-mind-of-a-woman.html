Discovering the Mind of a Woman

About the book

In Discovering the Mind of a Woman, Ken Nair explores how husbands can better understand their wives and respond with Christlike love. Drawing on his own experiences and those of other couples, he discusses communication, understanding, and responsibility within marriage.

By Ken Nair

Sherée’s Comment:

Although this book was written for men, I highly recommend it to women as well. It can help you feel seen and understood while offering a biblical perspective on the love, understanding, and care a Christlike husband is called to demonstrate. When I read it, I couldn’t put it down. I felt deeply seen, understood, and validated—and I hope it offers you that same encouragement.

A MUST READ.
