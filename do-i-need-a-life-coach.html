Do I need a Life Coach?

We usually will not take ourselves as far as we can go on our own or think as big as we need to create greater success in our lives on our own. We only know what we know. And just like the good intentions of getting a gym membership to become and/or stay healthy and fit, our intentions to learn how to receive more will often falter when the ego convinces us to stay in our comfort zone!  That is why we can’t change without new information, new actions and habits and new beliefs that are aligned with our vision. A trusted coach can stop us when we want to revert to the comfort zone area.

Having a coach who is trained to help you create clarity of your vision and spot your limiting patterns and beliefs will enable you to make the changes necessary for the results you desire. Coaches help people overcome their fears (F.E.A.R. -False Evidence Appearing Real) and will spot your self-sabotaging patterns if they are there. 

​A coach helps you get more from yourself than you ever thought you could.  A coach holds you to a much higher standard than you would yourself... encouraging you all the way.  In the long run, hiring a Life Coach saves a lot of money from making the wrong choices, saves a lot of time from going down wrong paths and saves a lot of stress, frustration and pain along the way also. The best investment you can make.... is in yourself.
  • If you're looking for someone to listen, understand and guide; while you look at your own situation, make your own decisions about what to do, have accountability and then take action steps.
  • If you feel like you're stuck in a situation and can't see a way out.
  • If you feel like you're in transition and want to know what the next step is?
  • If you feel like you've gotten off track somewhere and want to get back on track or even change your path.
  • If you desire more from life – in all its aspects, if you want to live more authentically and purposefully.
  • ​If you feel stuck in a relationship that is toxic.
  • If your relationship(s) could be better or aren't working but you don't know what to do to improve them.
  • If you're longing to broaden your vision, increase your confidence, enhance your relational and communication skills.
  • If you're looking for someone to challenge you to reach a big goal that you really want.
If you're ready to invest your time
and resources to achieve what you really want​......
