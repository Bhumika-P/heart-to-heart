Emergency Phone or Virtual Coaching

For established clients who have a pop-up situation and need to book an appointment right away, EMERGENCY PHONE CALL or EMERGENCY VIRTUAL (ZOOM/FACETIME) APPOINTMENTS are available.

This appointment would be scheduled sometime in the next 36 hours or less. Any appointment scheduled 48 hours or later would just be considered a standard phone call or virtual (ZOOM/FACETIME) appointment, non-emergency, at the standard fee.

If you are not able to find a EMERGENCY VIRTUAL (ZOOM/FACETIME) appointment on our calendar, schedule a EMERGENCY PHONE appointment instead. We may not be near a computer at that particular time.

Your appointment will be sent to the staff for confirmation. After your appointment is confirmed, you will be notified via email. If we can't make the exact time that you purchased, then you will receive a call from our office asking you for an alternate time closest to the time you requested. We should be able to fit you in.

***The virtual format we use is ZOOM or FACETIME. After we have established a time for this appointment, we will email you a link sometime before. Click on the link to join the meeting for your ZOOM appointment.
