The foundation of everything is our walk with God.  But how deep is it?  

Don't let pride answer that question.  
Answer from that secret place in your heart.

  • Can you hear the voice of the Holy Spirit when you need to at the time you need it?
  • ​Do you struggle with fear?
  • Do you have a deep intimate relationship with the Lord or do you want to go deeper but it just seems like you can't get there?  
  • Do you want to produce more fruits of love?
  • Do you know your gifts and calling?  ​

​As your coach, I may use techniques such as role-play, scripts and scenario-illustrations for practicing and processing.  I have created questionnaires to explore your strengths, values, passion, gifts, etc. Sometimes, depending on each individual case, some Emotional Healing sessions would be very good to explore.

Then Lord spoke to my heart and told me to go back to that dark hole and to reach down and help that next person that was trying to claw their way out.
