Marriage Restoration Retreat

COMING SOON!    (DON'T BOOK JUST YET)

Weekend Marriage Restoration Retreat:  This retreat is for married couples.  In this retreat couples will learn some key principles for healing and restoring their marriage.  Some of the greatest pain can come when marriages are broken.  Kevin and Sherée work with one couple at a time to give total undivided attention.  

Kevin and Sherée were married in 1991 after meeting at a High School Reunion.  After 10 years of a very rocky marriage, they were divorced.  Even though they went through numerous counselors, pastors, programs, marriage seminars, ministries, etc.  They both wanted the marriage to work but it just didn't.  Why?  Because through all churches and programs….no one was truly taking the time to go back to ALL the roots of the emotional wounds that were there in the first place. AND ON TOP OF THAT, there is very little Biblical teachings/discipleship for men in how to be Christ-like husbands and the spiritual leaders in their home. What does God expect from a husband?  We have all heard the watered down sermons and maybe even a few good ones…..but did any one teach a step by step on what is truly expected?  If a man did not have good role models growing up, how is he to know what to do?  ​ We can not give what we don't have.

Kevin and Sherée are just one couple that experienced the death of a marriage unfortunately because of this.  There are thousands and thousands of stories just like theirs.  But through all the hurt and pain, there was a very tiny ember still in their hearts for each other.  After years of trying, even after a divorce, the discovery was made.  This discovery changed their lives.  Kevin and Sherée remarried and now have a passion to help others.  

Our retreats are built on the foundational truths taught during the Marriage Restoration Retreat.  It provides a safe place to help you walk through grieving losses in your life, healing from the pain from emotional needs not met when you were a child, and removing the glass walls in your life that keep you from moving forward.  Our teaching and workbook will help you work through your present relationship difficulties in your life and in general.

At this retreat, you will be provided a private bedroom with an attached bathroom when staying plus dinner on Friday, 3 meals on Saturday, and breakfast on Sunday (included in the fee).  If you have a special diet, please bring what you need, there is plenty of refrigeration for your food.  Also feel free to bring your favorite snacks and drinks.  We have coffee and many choices of teas.

PLEASE NOTE: There is an 8 pound dog on the premises if you have any allergies.

Check-In time will be on Friday at 3:00pm and goes threw to Check-Out time on Sunday at 10:00am. There will be a small church service on Sunday at 11:00am that is part of the retreat for you to attend with Kevin and Sherée a few miles away from where you will be staying.

​Please feel free to call us with any of your questions.
