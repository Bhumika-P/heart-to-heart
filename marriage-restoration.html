Marriage Restoration

Marriage/ Relationship Restoration

Kevin and Sherée also maintain a private Life Coaching practice where they coach married couples and others in restoring relationships via Skype, Phone, FaceTime or In-Office appointments under the name ﻿Heart to Heart Life Coaching LLC﻿.  Whether you want to better your marriage, save a marriage that is heading for the divorce courts, restore a marriage that ended in divorce, reconcile a relationship within family or friendship; they have been-there-and-done-that and can walk you through the process of restoration.
2 Corinthians 5:18
New International Version (NIV)
18 All this is from God, who reconciled us to Himself through Christ 
and gave us the ministry of reconciliation
Kevin and Sheree were married in 1991 after meeting at a High School Reunion.  After 10 years of a rocky marriage, they were divorced.  Even though they went through numerous counselors, pastors, programs, marriage seminars, ministries, etc.  They both wanted the marriage to work but it just didn't.  Why?  Because through all churches and programs….no one was teaching men how to be Christ-like in a marriage relationship.  Kevin and Sheree are just one couple that experienced the death of a marriage unfortunately because of this.  There are hundreds and maybe in reality thousands that are just like them.  

But through all this, there was still an ember there in their hearts for each other.  After years of trying, even after a divorce, the discovery was made.  This discovery changed their lives.  


What does God expect from a husband?  We have all heard the watered down sermons and maybe even a few good ones…..but did any one teach a step by step on what is truly expected?  If a man did not have good role models growing up, how is he to know what to do?  ​
