Phone or Virtual Coaching

Phone and virtual coaching offer a convenient way to receive personalized support without traveling. You can connect with your coach from home, your office, or another quiet, private place where you feel comfortable and can focus on your session.

Appointments are available by Phone, Zoom, or FaceTime. For virtual appointments, we will email you the meeting information before your scheduled session.

The Benefits of Phone vs. Virtual Coaching

Both phone and virtual appointments offer personalized coaching without traveling. The main difference is whether you prefer an audio-only conversation or seeing your coach during the session.

Phone appointments

  • Simple to join. Talk by phone without setting up a video connection.
  • Less screen time. Focus on the conversation without watching a screen.
  • Less concern about appearance. You don’t need to think about lighting, your background, or being on camera.
  • May feel more comfortable for personal conversations. Some people find it easier to speak openly without being seen.
  • Works without an internet connection. A standard phone call requires reliable phone service.

Virtual appointments — Zoom or FaceTime

  • Face-to-face connection. See your coach while talking from separate locations.
  • Visual communication. Facial expressions and gestures can add context to what is being shared.
  • A more familiar meeting experience. Video may feel closer to sitting together in person.
  • May help you feel more connected. Seeing your coach’s responses can offer reassurance during personal conversations.
  • Uses an internet connection. You’ll need a compatible device, camera, microphone, and reliable internet access.

Which is right for you? Choose the format that helps you feel most comfortable, connected, and able to focus. Both work best when you have a quiet, private place where you can speak freely without interruptions.
