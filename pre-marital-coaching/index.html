Pre-Marital Coaching

This is an exciting time in your life.  Most every couple have so much wonderful expectations for this new path.  However, real life sets in way to fast and success depends on what tools you have in your tool box.  Some of us are fortunate to start out with some but the sad truth is that most of us start out with very few.  No matter how many tools you started out with, wouldn't it be great to be able to walk through this new life relationally with more?  

Also, if you have been married before and of course don't want to go through the heartache again of a divorce, this type of coaching would be a great way to start your new marriage!

​BENEFITS:
  • How to have healthy communication
  • How to fight fairly
  • How to keep the marriage as a priority
  • How to have FUN
  • How to work together as parents
  • and more!!


YOUR KIDS ARE GETTING MARRIED?
  • How can you best help your children now so they can have a successful marriage?   This will be the gift that keeps on giving.
  • What better gift can you give them?   Set them up for success and beat the odds of the 50/50 divorce rate.
  • What if they decide to have children?   Divorce sets those kids (your grandchildren) up for so much heartache and failure in their lives.
  • Is this your children's second marriage?   75% of all second marriages end up in divorce.  Help your kids to stay out of those stats!
