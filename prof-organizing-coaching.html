Professional Organizing Coaching

You may have tried getting yourself organized in the past but things went back to the way they were; which can be very frustrating.  There are reasons why this happens.  We realize each client is a unique individual.  This being said also means that each person has a preferred style of organization.  We work with our clients to discover their particular style and then develop a plan virtually.

I am a little different from most professional organizers because I first and foremost focus on "coaching" the client.  Through my years of experience as a Hands-On Organizer, I have seen where things go south.  Other organizers may say that they do coach the client but I have seen that this is not necessarily true.  When the organizers leave the home looks beautiful but the client truly does not know how to continue with the process.  To me, this is failing to give the client the best service possible. I prefer to teach the client how not to need me anymore.

​There is another way to get organized also….Virtually.

Virtual Organizing is where a Professional Organizer connects with you over the phone, Skype or FaceTime and gives you a game plan and teaches you how to tackle your areas of need.  Virtual Organizing will save you a substantial amount of money vs. Hands-On Organizing because you will be completing the work yourself with the guidance of a Professional Organizer.  See Virtual Organizing vs. Hands-on Organizing here.

Professional Organizing Coaching is not just about cleaning your closets or organizing your kitchen.  I can also work with you on Personal Productivity….like areas of time management, grocery lists/meal planning, apps to help you stay organized, errands/schedules, etc.

Before you think that Virtual Organizing is not for you, please, please CLICK HERE and read more information about it or give Sherée a call.   We think you will be very surprised.

Professional Organizing Coaches use tested principles and expertise to enhance the lives of their clients….by designing custom organizing systems and teaching organizing skills, they help individuals take control of their surroundings, their time, their paper piles and their lives!  ​
