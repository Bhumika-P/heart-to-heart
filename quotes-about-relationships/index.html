Quotes about Love and Relationships:


“Whenever you’re in conflict with someone, there is one factor that can make the difference between damaging your relationship and deepening it. That factor is attitude.”
– William James

“Friendship is born at that moment when one person says to another, ‘What! You too? I thought I was the only one”
— C.S. Lewis

"Darkness cannot drive out darkness; only light can do that. Hate cannot drive out hate; only love can do that." 
-Martin Luther King, Jr.

"Nothing can bring a real sense of security into the home except true love." 
-Billy Graham

"Love begins by taking care of the closest ones - the ones at home."

“Assumptions are the termites of relationships.

– Catherine Pulsifer

“Real love takes work. You have to be willing to make the effort.” 
—Rabbi Weinberg

“What most people need to learn in life is how to love people and use things instead of using people and loving things.”
-Unknown

"Loving others always costs us something and requires effort. And you have to decide to do it on purpose. You can't wait for a feeling to motivate you."
-Joyce Meyer
