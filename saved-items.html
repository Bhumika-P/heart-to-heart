It’s very natural to desire safety and intimacy within our marriage and close relationships but oftentimes we deny who we are in order to be accepted, in essence we betray ourselves. Or there are times we might hide behind masks and barriers and this cuts off our ability to engage in a deeper connection and closeness. The people we are the most intimate with are those who push our “buttons” the most. Sometimes those people can be our greatest teachers if we learn to understand why we are reacting to them the way we do.

It addresses entrenched thoughts, behaviors, emotions and blocks… is what Sherée calls “Glass Walls”. The process involves talking and taking simple actions; focusing on your goals rather than the problems.

Relationship Coaching will help you understand yourself and your relational patterns. This type of coaching doesn’t need to be “Hard Work”, it needs to be the “Right Work”.

If you are looking for the ability to be successful in your relationships; Relationship Coaching will teach you the skills needed to facilitate change, reduce the stress and gain happiness and harmony.
