Training

﻿Because people are so busy these days, Life Coaches from all over the world
do most of their one-on-one training sessions over the internet. 
Training over the phone allows the coachee(s) to experience one-to-one training
​without having to travel.  
​Many people we work with find Training over the Phone an ideal alternative.
The Benefits of Training by Phone
​
  • Training by Phone will give you the customized one-on-one training and support you are looking for to learn how to coach others.
 
  • Training by Phone is extremely time effective.
    • You will not waste any of your valuable time traveling back and forth to training meetings.
    • You won’t get stuck in traffic. 
    • You won't have to line up a babysitter.
    • You won't have to battle the weather.
 
  • Training by Phone can be cost effective in some cases.
    • ​Cost for your gas/tolls.
    • Cost for your babysitter.
 
  • ​Training by Phone allows more flexibility in scheduling training sessions also. 
​
  • You can enjoy your training in the surroundings of your choice.
    • You can choose a quiet, private and relaxed place to be during our private training sessions.
    • You will be able to connect with us from the comfort and privacy of your own home or office or wherever you feel most comfortable.​
Training by Phone also enables us to work with clients who would not otherwise be able to benefit from our one-on-one training services
​that are out of our area, state or country.
