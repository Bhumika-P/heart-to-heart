ZOOM/FACETIME/ Appointments

﻿Because people are so busy these days,
Life Coaches from all over the world do most of their coaching sessions over the internet. 
Virtual Coaching allows the coachee(s) to experience one-to-one coaching without having to travel.  
​Many people we work with find ZOOM/FACETIME coaching an ideal alternative.

We also do coaching by Phone.
The Benefits of Virtual Coaching
​
  • Coaching by SKYPE/ZOOM will give you the support and feedback you need for creating meaningful changes in your life and achieve the results you want; while still being able to connect on a very personal level with your Life Coach. It feels very much like face-to-face.
 
  • Virtual Coaching is extremely time effective.
    • You will not waste any of your valuable time traveling back and forth to our coaching meetings.
    • You won’t get stuck in traffic. 
    • You won't have to line up a babysitter.
    • You won't have to battle the weather.
 
  • Virtual Coaching can be cost effective in some cases.
    • ​Cost for your gas/tolls.
    • Cost for your babysitter.
 
  • ​Virtual Coaching over the ZOOM/FACETIME allows more flexibility in scheduling coaching sessions also. 
​
  • You can enjoy coaching in the surroundings of your choice.
    • You can choose a quiet, private and relaxed place to be during our private coaching sessions.
    • You will be able to connect with us from the comfort and privacy of your own home or office or wherever you feel most comfortable.​
Working virtually also enables us to work with clients who would not otherwise be able to benefit
​from our coaching services that are out of our area, state or country.
